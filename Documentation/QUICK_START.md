# CRM Starz Morocco - Quick Start Guide (5 Minute Intro)

## Welcome! Let's Get You Started in 5 Minutes ⚡

### Step 1: Login (30 seconds)
```
URL: http://127.0.0.1:8000/
Email: admin@crm-starz.dev
Password: [provided by admin]
```
✅ **Then:** Click "Sign In"

### Step 2: Change Your Password (1 minute)
1. Click your **profile icon** (top-right)
2. Select **Settings**
3. Enter new password (confirm twice)
4. Click **Save**

### Step 3: Explore the Dashboard (1 minute)
You'll see:
- 📊 Total Companies
- 👥 Total Contacts  
- 📈 Activities This Month
- 🎯 Pending Leads

**Click each card** to explore!

### Step 4: Add Your First Company (1 minute)
1. Click **Companies** in left menu
2. Click **Add Company**
3. Enter company name
4. Select sector (e.g., "Automotive")
5. Click **Save Company**

### Step 5: Add a Contact (1 minute)
1. Click **Contacts** in left menu
2. Click **Add Contact**
3. Enter name and email
4. Select the company you just created
5. Click **Save Contact**

### Step 6: Log an Activity (1 minute)
1. Click **Activities** in left menu
2. Click **Log Activity**
3. Select: Call, Email, or Meeting
4. Pick the contact you just added
5. Type a quick note
6. Click **Log Activity**

## Common First Tasks

### Add More Companies
**Companies** → **Add Company** → Fill fields → **Save**

### Add More Contacts  
**Contacts** → **Add Contact** → Pick company → **Save**

### Log Activities Frequently
**Activities** → **Log Activity** → Every interaction matters!

### Review Leads
**Leads** → **Lead Review** → **Approve** or **Deny**

### Send Email
**Email Campaigns** → **Create Campaign** → Pick contacts → **Send**
