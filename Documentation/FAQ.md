# CRM Starz Morocco - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## General Questions

### Q: What exactly is this CRM?
**A:** This is an **Account-Based Marketing (ABM) CRM** designed specifically for Moroccan manufacturing companies. It helps you:
- Track companies in Moroccan free zones
- Log every interaction (calls, emails, meetings)
- Discover new prospects
- Manage quotes and RFQs
- Run email campaigns
- Monitor engagement

Think of it as: **Salesforce + Lead Intelligence + Email Marketing** in one system, optimized for Morocco.

### Q: Who should use this system?
**A:** Anyone in sales, marketing, or business development who:
- Needs to track company interactions
- Manages multiple prospects
- Wants to organize sales pipeline
- Needs activity logging for compliance

### Q: How is this different from Excel?
**A:** 
| Feature | Excel | This CRM |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Track activities | Manual | Automatic |
| Search companies | Slow | Instant |
| Email campaigns | None | Built-in |
| Lead scoring | None | Automatic |
| Reports | Manual | Real-time |
| Collaboration | Email chains | Central hub |
| Security | Risky | Encrypted |

## Companies & Contacts

### Q: How do I find a company I already added?
**A:** Three ways:
1. **Search bar** (top) - Type company name
2. **Companies list** - Use filters (sector, region, tier)
3. **Timeline** - Scroll through history

**Tip:** Bookmark companies you use most!

### Q: Can I have two companies with the same name?
**A:** No - the system prevents duplicates. If you see a duplicate message:
1. Search for existing company
2. Use that company instead of creating new
3. Update info if needed

### Q: How do I add multiple contacts at the company?
**A:** 
1. Go to **Contacts** → **Add Contact**
2. Enter first person's details
3. Click **Save Contact**
4. Repeat for each person
5. All are linked to same company

### Q: How do I update a company's phone number?
**A:** 
1. Go to **Companies** → search for company
2. Click company name
3. Click **Edit**
4. Update the phone field
5. Click **Save Changes**

### Q: Can I see all contacts at a company?
**A:** Yes! Two ways:
1. Go to company detail page - see "Contacts" section
2. Go to **Contacts** → filter by company

### Q: How do I mark a contact as "no longer there"?
**A:** Edit the contact:
1. Click contact name
2. Click **Edit**
3. Change title to "[Left Company]" or add note
4. Click **Save**

*Better than deleting!*

### Q: Can I import existing companies from Excel?
**A:** Currently, you must enter manually. Future versions will support bulk import. Contact admin if you have many to add.

## Activities & Logging

### Q: Why should I log every activity?
**A:** Because:
- ✅ Engagement scores depend on it (activity = engagement)
- ✅ Team members see your work
- ✅ Compliance/audit trail
- ✅ Better reporting
- ✅ Never forget what you discussed

### Q: What counts as an activity?
**A:** Log these:
- ✅ Phone calls (any length)
- ✅ Emails sent
- ✅ In-person meetings
- ✅ Video calls
- ✅ LinkedIn messages
- ✅ Follow-ups
- ❌ Don't log: Reading emails, internal tasks

### Q: Can I edit an activity I already logged?
**A:** Once saved, you **cannot edit** (for audit trail). If you made a mistake:
1. Log a new activity with correct info
2. Note the error in the description
3. Contact admin if needed for deletion

### Q: How far back can I see activities?
**A:** All activities from day 1 are stored. No limit!

### Q: Can I see all activities I logged?
**A:** Yes!
1. Go to **Activities** → **Activity Log**
2. Filter by your name if needed
3. Sort by date
4. All your interactions shown

### Q: What does "outcome" mean?
**A:** When you log an activity, choose the outcome:
- **Successful** - Good conversation, moving forward
- **Follow-up Required** - Need to contact again
- **Not Interested** - Company declined / lost interest

This helps track next steps!

## Leads Management

### Q: What's the difference between a Lead and a Company?
**A:**
| Aspect | Lead | Company |
|--------|------|---------|
| Status | Under review | In system |
| Approval needed | Yes | No |
| Engagement tracked | Limited | Yes |
| Activities logged | Few | Many |
| In your pipeline | Not yet | Yes |

### Q: Where do leads come from?
**A:** Leads can be:
1. **System-discovered** - Webcrawler found them
2. **Manually added** - You entered them
3. **Imported** - Batch upload

### Q: How do I know if a lead is good?
**A:** Look at:
- **Lead Score** (0-100, higher = better)
- **Company information** - Does it match your sector?
- **Website quality** - Does it look legitimate?
- **Region** - Is it in a free zone?

### Q: What if a lead has wrong information?
**A:** You can:
1. **Deny it** - Mark as not suitable
2. **Approve it anyway** - Fix data after conversion
3. **Edit before conversion** - Some fields are editable

### Q: Can I convert a lead manually?
**A:** Yes!
1. Find the lead
2. Click **Approve**
3. Click **Convert to Company**
4. Data transfers to companies system
5. You can now edit more fields

### Q: What if I accidentally denied a lead?
**A:** Denied leads are stored but hidden. Contact your admin to recover.

### Q: How often should I review leads?
**A:** At least **once per week**. Leads pile up if you wait!

### Q: Can I assign leads to team members?
**A:** Yes!
1. In Lead Review page
2. Click **Assign** on a lead
3. Pick team member
4. They see it in their queue

## Email Campaigns

### Q: Can I send an email to one person?
**A:** Yes, use Email Campaigns with one recipient:
1. Go to **Email Campaigns** → **Create Campaign**
2. Pick just one contact
3. Enter subject and message
4. Click **Send Now**

### Q: How do I know if someone opened my email?
**A:** After sending:
1. Go to **Email Campaigns** → campaign name
2. See who opened (shows open time)
3. See who clicked links
4. Track overall open rate

### Q: Can I schedule emails for later?
**A:** Yes!
1. Create campaign
2. Click **Schedule**
3. Pick date and time
4. Emails send automatically

### Q: How many emails should I send?
**A:** Best practices:
- ✅ 1 email per contact per week max
- ✅ Segment by company/sector
- ✅ Personalize subject lines
- ✅ Include clear call-to-action
- ❌ Don't spam (multiple per day)

### Q: Can I see email templates?
**A:** Yes! Templates come pre-loaded. You can:
1. Use as-is
2. Customize before sending
3. Create new templates

### Q: How do I unsubscribe someone from emails?
**A:** In contact details:
1. Click the contact
2. Toggle **Unsubscribed** 
3. Save

*(This is important for compliance!)*

### Q: What's the open rate benchmark?
**A:** 
- Average: 20-30%
- Good: 30-40%
- Excellent: 40%+

If below 15%, try different subject lines.

## Quotes & RFQs

### Q: What's the difference between a Quote and RFQ?
**A:**
| Feature | Quote | RFQ |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Who starts | You | Customer |
| Direction | You → Customer | Customer → You |
| Response | Waiting | Urgent |
| Track | Yes | Yes |

### Q: How do I create a quote?
**A:** 
1. Go to **Quotes** → **Create Quote**
2. Select company
3. Add line items (product, qty, price)
4. Review total
5. Click **Send to Company**

### Q: Can I create a quote PDF?
**A:** Yes!
1. Click **Generate PDF** before sending
2. PDF opens in new tab
3. You can print or download
4. Send via email manually if needed

### Q: How long is a quote valid?
**A:** By default, 30 days. You can:
1. Edit quote details
2. Change expiration date
3. Customers see it in their portal

### Q: What if a customer asks to modify the quote?
**A:** You can:
1. Edit quote details
2. Add/remove line items
3. Update pricing
4. Send updated version
5. Customer gets notification

### Q: How do I know if a customer viewed my quote?
**A:** 
1. Go to **Quotes** page
2. See "Viewed" timestamp
3. Or click quote for full details
4. Shows exactly when they opened it

### Q: What's an RFQ?
**A:** "Request for Quote" - customer asks you for pricing:
1. You receive it in **RFQ Pipeline**
2. You respond with quote
3. Customer reviews
4. You negotiate or close

### Q: How do I respond to an RFQ?
**A:** 
1. Go to **RFQ Pipeline**
2. Click the RFQ
3. Click **Create Quote**
4. Fill in your pricing
5. Send to customer

### Q: What if we need an NDA for an RFQ?
**A:** Mark it in the RFQ:
1. Click **RFQ**
2. Check **NDA Required**
3. Note signature date once received
4. Flag for legal team if needed

## Troubleshooting

### Q: I'm seeing a blank page
**A:** Try:
1. Refresh browser (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
2. Clear cache (browser settings)
3. Try different browser
4. Check internet connection

### Q: My changes didn't save
**A:** Check:
1. Are there error messages? (red boxes)
2. Did you fill all required fields?
3. Try saving again
4. Refresh page and check

### Q: I can't find a company I added
**A:** Try:
1. Refresh the page
2. Search by name (use `/` key)
3. Use filters (sector, region)
4. Check spelling (names are exact match)

### Q: An activity won't log
**A:** Make sure:
1. You selected a **Contact** ✅
2. Contact has a **Company** ✅
3. You filled in **Description** ✅
4. You selected **Activity Type** ✅

### Q: The system is slow
**A:** Try:
1. Clear browser cache
2. Close other tabs
3. Try a different browser
4. Refresh page
5. Contact admin if still slow

### Q: I see an error message
**A:** Common errors:
- "Company name already exists" → Search first!
- "Email already exists" → Use different email
- "Required field missing" → Fill all marked fields
- Other errors → Screenshot and email admin
