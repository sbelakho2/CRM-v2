The Big Picture

  • It replaces scattered sales activity with one connected journey: find a prospect, qualify them, raise an RFQ, win the quote, and keep the customer.
  • Growth engines (ABM, email campaigns, web discovery) feed straight into execution engines (tasks, meetings, pipeline control) — no copy-paste between tools.
  • Every action leaves a trace, so day-to-day work turns into measurable signals you can actually report on and improve.
